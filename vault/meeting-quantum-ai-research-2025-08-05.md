## Key Discussion Points

### Quantum Supremacy Progress
Dr. Chen presented the latest results from IBM's quantum team:
- **1000+ qubit systems** now operational
- **Error rates** below 0.1% for certain operations
- **Coherence times** extended to 100+ microseconds

Prof. Rodriguez raised concerns about:
- Real-world applications still limited
- Quantum advantage mainly in specific problems
- Need for better error correction

### AI Efficiency Research
Dr. Wang shared fascinating results on neural network pruning:
- **90% parameter reduction** with minimal accuracy loss
- Novel attention mechanisms inspired by quantum entanglement
- Energy consumption reduced by 75%

### Hybrid Approaches Discussion
The most exciting part of the meeting was brainstorming hybrid systems:

**Quantum-Enhanced Training**:
- Use quantum computers for hyperparameter optimization
- Quantum sampling for better data augmentation
- Quantum-inspired optimization algorithms

**Classical-Quantum Interfaces**:
- Classical neural networks with quantum processing layers
- Quantum feature maps for high-dimensional data
- Error-corrected quantum subroutines
