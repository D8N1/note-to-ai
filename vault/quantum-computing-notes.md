# Quantum Computing Research Notes

## Overview
Quantum computing leverages quantum mechanical phenomena like superposition and entanglement to process information in fundamentally different ways than classical computers.

## Key Concepts

### Quantum Bits (Qubits)
- Unlike classical bits (0 or 1), qubits can exist in superposition
- Can represent both 0 and 1 simultaneously until measured
- Enables parallel computation on exponentially larger state spaces

### Quantum Entanglement
- Two or more qubits become correlated
- Measuring one qubit instantly affects its entangled partners
- Einstein called this "spooky action at a distance"

### Quantum Gates
- Basic operations that manipulate qubits
- Examples: Hadamard gate, CNOT gate, Pauli gates
- Quantum circuits built by combining these gates

## Current Research Areas

### Quantum Algorithms
- **Shor's Algorithm**: Factoring large numbers efficiently
- **Grover's Algorithm**: Searching unsorted databases quadratically faster
- **Quantum Machine Learning**: Quantum neural networks and optimization

### Hardware Approaches
- **Superconducting qubits**: IBM, Google approach
- **Trapped ions**: IonQ, Honeywell approach  
- **Photonic quantum**: PsiQuantum, Xanadu approach
- **Topological qubits**: Microsoft approach

## Challenges
- Quantum decoherence and noise
- Error correction requirements
- Scalability issues
- Need for extremely low temperatures

## Recent Breakthroughs
- Google's quantum supremacy claim (2019)
- IBM's 1000+ qubit roadmap
- Quantum error correction demonstrations
